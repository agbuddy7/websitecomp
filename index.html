A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.

Does this replace the main chat?+

No. Keep chatting in the stream as usual. Roommate adds a small conversation around a shared moment while the main chat keeps moving.

Do I get joined automatically?+

Never. You choose whether to accept each invitation. You can also dismiss it or mute the topic.

Why do I need to share my identity?+

With your permission, Twitch confirms which chat messages belong to you so Roommate can deliver your invitations. The Extension asks you to link your identity before matching invitations.

Can I install it right now?+

Roommate is currently a hackathon prototype in Twitch Hosted Test. You can explore the live Signal Lab and the interactive example here.
